## Question 3: What is 7 ÷ 0.1? (MULTIPLE_CHOICE)
**Correct Answer:** 70  

**Explanation / Steps:**  
1. 0.1 means “one‑tenth.”  
2. Dividing by a number is the same as multiplying by its reciprocal.  
3. The reciprocal of 0.1 is 10 (because 1 ÷ 0.1 = 10).  
4. So, 7 ÷ 0.1 = 7 × 10.  
5. 7 × 10 = 70.  

## Feedback  
Great job on spotting the most common answer! Remember that dividing by a decimal can be easier if you turn it into multiplication. Keep practicing this trick!

## Common Mistakes  
- Thinking 7 ÷ 0.1 equals 0.7 (mixing up the direction of the operation).  
- Forgetting that 0.1 is the same as 1 ÷ 10 and not just “one tenth.”  

## Keep Practicing  
Try a few more problems like 5 ÷ 0.2 or 8 ÷ 0.5. Remember: turn the decimal into a fraction and then multiply. Good luck!

## Question 4: Multiply 5.6 by 0.1. (MULTIPLE_CHOICE)

**Explanation / Steps:**  
1. Write 5.6 as 56 ÷ 10.

4. Divide that result by another 10 to get 0.56.

2. Reading 0.1 as 1 instead of 1/10.  
3. Mixing up the order of operations with division and multiplication.

## Keep Practicing
