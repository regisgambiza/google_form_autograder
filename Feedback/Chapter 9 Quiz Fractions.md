## Question 3: What is 1 1/2 + 1 3/4? (MULTIPLE_CHOICE)
**Correct Answer:** 3 ¼  

**Explanation / Steps:**  
1. Write each mixed number as an improper fraction.  
   - 1 ½ = 3⁄2  
   - 1 ¾ = 7⁄4  
2. Choose a common denominator (4).  
   - 3⁄2 = 6⁄4  
   - 7⁄4 stays 7⁄4  
3. Add the fractions.  
   - 6⁄4 + 7⁄4 = 13⁄4  
4. Convert back to a mixed number.  
   - 13⁄4 = 3 ¼  

So, 1 ½ + 1 ¾ = **3 ¼**.

## Feedback  
Great job! Most of you got it right. Keep practicing how to change mixed numbers to improper fractions before adding.

## Common Mistakes  
1. Forgetting to find a common denominator.  
2. Adding the whole numbers and fractions separately instead of converting first.  
3. Leaving the answer as an improper fraction instead of turning it back into a mixed number.

## Keep Practicing  
Try adding 2 ¾ and 3 ⅜. Remember to convert each mixed number to an improper fraction first, then find a common denominator, add, and convert back. Good luck!

## Question 4: Find the sum of 1 7/12 and 3/4. (MULTIPLE_CHOICE)
**Correct Answer:** 2 1/3  

**Explanation / Steps:**  
1. Convert the mixed number to an improper fraction.  
   - \(1 \tfrac{7}{12} = \frac{12+7}{12} = \frac{19}{12}\).  
2. Convert \( \frac34 \) to have the same denominator.  
   - \(\frac34 = \frac{9}{12}\).  
3. Add the numerators.  
   - \(\frac{19}{12} + \frac{9}{12} = \frac{28}{12}\).  
4. Simplify the result.  
   - \(\frac{28}{12} = 2 \frac{4}{12} = 2 \frac{1}{3}\).

So the correct answer is **4 1/8**.
