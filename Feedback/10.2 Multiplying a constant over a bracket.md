## Question 3: Expand 6(2x + 4). (MULTIPLE_CHOICE)
**Correct Answer:** 12x + 24  

**Explanation / Steps:**  
1. Take the 6 and multiply it by the first term inside the parentheses: 6 × 2x = 12x.  
2. Take the 6 and multiply it by the second term: 6 × 4 = 24.  
3. Add the two products together: 12x + 24.  

## Feedback  
Great job! Most of you found the correct answer. Keep up the good work—distributing is a key skill in algebra.

## Common Mistakes  
- **Missing a term:** Some answers were 12x + 12 because 6 was multiplied by 2x but not correctly by 4.  
- **Wrong multiplication:** 6 × 4 = 24, not 12.  

## Keep Practicing  
Try expanding expressions like 5(3x + 7) and 4(2x – 3). Practice will make the steps feel automatic!

## Question 4: Expand 5(3y - 6). (MULTIPLE_CHOICE)
**Correct Answer:** 15y – 30  

**Explanation / Steps:**  
1. Write the expression: 5(3y – 6).  
2. Multiply 5 by 3y → 5 × 3y = 15y.  
3. Multiply 5 by –6 → 5 × (–6) = –30.  
4. Combine the two results: 15y – 30.  

## Feedback  
Great job! Most of you got it right. One student chose 30y + 15 – that shows a small slip with the signs. Keep an eye on the minus sign inside the parentheses.  

## Common Mistakes  
1. Forgetting to change the sign of the second term when multiplying by a negative number.  
2. Mixing up the order of multiplication (e.g., multiplying 5 by 6 instead of –6).  

## Keep Practicing  
Try expanding 7(2x – 4) and 3(5y + 8). Notice how the sign changes when you multiply by a negative number. Keep up the good work!
