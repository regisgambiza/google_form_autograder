## Question 4: Which sample is most representative for a school with 400 boys and 100 girls? (MULTIPLE_CHOICE)
**Correct Answer:** 80 boys and 20 girls chosen randomly  

**Explanation / Steps:**  
1. Count the whole school: 400 boys + 100 girls = 500 students.  
2. Find the share of each gender:  
   - Boys = 400 ÷ 500 = 0.8 (80 %).  
   - Girls = 100 ÷ 500 = 0.2 (20 %).  
3. Take a sample of 100 students.  
4. Apply the same shares:  
   - 80 % of 100 = 80 boys.  
   - 20 % of 100 = 20 girls.  

So a random sample of **80 boys and 20 girls** best represents the school’s gender ratio.

## Feedback  
Great job! You picked the right answer and understood how to keep the sample’s proportions the same as the whole class.

## Common Mistakes  
- Choosing a sample that doesn’t keep the 4:1 boy‑to‑girl ratio.  
- Forgetting to multiply the 80 % and 20 % by the sample size.  
- Picking an arbitrary number of students instead of 100.

## Keep Practicing  
Try calculating a representative sample for a different class size. Remember: find the total, determine each gender’s share, then apply that share to the sample size. Happy learning!

## Question 9: Which sampling method is systematic? (MULTIPLE_CHOICE)
**Correct Answer:** Choosing every 10th person on a list  

**Explanation / Steps:**  
1. Write all people in a list.  
2. Pick one starting person at random.  
3. From that person, count forward 10 people each time.  
4. Choose every 10th person until you finish the list.  
5. This regular 10‑person interval is what makes it systematic sampling.  

## Feedback  
Great job! You understand that systematic sampling follows a clear pattern. Keep practicing this idea with different numbers—like every 5th or every 20th person—to feel comfortable with the method.  

## Common Mistakes  
- Choosing names from a hat (that is random sampling, not systematic).  
- Only asking people from one class (doesn’t use a regular interval).  
- Forgetting to start from a random point before counting.  

## Keep Practicing  
Try a short activity: write a list of 30 classmates, choose a random start, and pick every 5th name. Check that you use the same interval each time. Good luck!
