## Question 3: What is 2 1/4 + 2 1/4? (MULTIPLE_CHOICE)
**Correct Answer:** 4 ½  

**Explanation / Steps:**  
1. Write each mixed number as an improper fraction.  
   - \(2 \frac{1}{4} = \frac{9}{4}\).  
   - The other one is the same: \(\frac{9}{4}\).  
2. Add the fractions:  
   \(\frac{9}{4} + \frac{9}{4} = \frac{18}{4}\).  
3. Simplify the fraction:  
   \(\frac{18}{4} = \frac{9}{2}\).  
4. Convert back to a mixed number:  
   \(\frac{9}{2} = 4 \frac{1}{2}\).  

**Feedback**  
Great job! You understood how to add mixed numbers and got the answer right.

**Common Mistakes**  
- Forgetting to convert mixed numbers into improper fractions before adding.  
- Mixing up the numerators or denominators while adding.  
- Leaving the result as a fraction instead of converting it back to a mixed number.  

**Keep Practicing**  
Try adding mixed numbers that have different denominators, and remember to find a common denominator first. Keep up the good work!

## Question 4: Find the sum of 3 2/3 and 3 2/3. (MULTIPLE_CHOICE)
**Correct Answer:** 7 1/3  

**Explanation / Steps:**
1. Add the whole numbers: 3 + 3 = 6.  
2. Add the fractions: 2/3 + 2/3 = 4/3.  
3. Convert 4/3 to a mixed number: 4/3 = 1 1/3.  
4. Add the 1 from step 3 to the whole‑number sum: 6 + 1 = 7.  
5. Keep the remaining 1/3.  
6. The final sum is **7 1/3**.

## Question 5: Add 4 2/3 and 4 1/2. (MULTIPLE_CHOICE)
**Correct Answer:** 9 1/6  

**Explanation / Steps:**  
1. Convert each mixed number to an improper fraction.  
   * 4 2/3 = 4 + 2/3 = 12/3 + 2/3 = 14/3  
   * 4 1/2 = 4 + 1/2 = 8/2 + 1/2 = 9/2  
2. Find a common denominator (the least common multiple of 3 and 2 is 6).  
   * 14/3 = 28/6  
   * 9/2  = 27/6  
3. Add the numerators.  
   * 28 + 27 = 55 → 55/6  
4. Convert back to a mixed number.  
   * 55 ÷ 6 = 9 with remainder 1 → 9 1/6  

**Feedback**  
Great job on most of your answers! You’re getting the right idea of adding fractions. Keep practicing the steps and you’ll get even faster.

**Common Mistakes**

**Keep Practicing**  
Try writing every mixed number as an improper fraction first—this makes the addition easier. Remember to check your final answer by converting back to a mixed number. Happy learning!
