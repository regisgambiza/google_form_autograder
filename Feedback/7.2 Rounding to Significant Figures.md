## Question 3: Round 32401 to 2 significant figures. (MULTIPLE_CHOICE)
**Correct Answer:** 32000  

**Explanation / Steps:**  
1. Look at the first two digits of 32401. They are 3 and 2.  
2. Check the next digit (the third one). It is 4.  
3. Since 4 is less than 5, we do **not** increase the second digit.  
4. Change all remaining digits to zeros.  
5. The rounded number is 32000.

## Question 39: Why can the same number have different numbers of significant figures depending on how it is written or rounded? (SHORT_ANSWER)
**Correct Answer:**  
Because writing or rounding changes the shown precision.

**Explanation / Steps:**
1. Write the number in a particular way (for example, 100, 100.0, 100.00).  
2. Count the digits that are significant in that written form.  
3. If you round the number or change its notation (add or remove trailing zeros, move the decimal point), the count of significant digits changes.  
4. Therefore the same numeric value can have different numbers of significant figures depending on how it is written or rounded.

## Feedback

## Common Mistakes  
- Thinking the number of digits is always the same, regardless of notation.  
- Forgetting that trailing zeros after a decimal point are significant.  
- Overlooking how rounding can add or remove significant digits.
