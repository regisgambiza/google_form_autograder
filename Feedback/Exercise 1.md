## Question 6: d (SHORT_ANSWER)
**Correct Answer:** 135  

**Explanation / Steps:**
1. In any quadrilateral, all four interior angles add up to 360°.  
2. Two of the angles are already given: 120° and 105°.  
3. Subtract those two angles from 360°:  
   \(360° - 120° - 105° = 360° - 225° = 135°\).  
4. Therefore the remaining angle, D, equals **135°**.

## Feedback  
Great job! Most of you got the right answer. Remember to always subtract the known angles from 360° when solving for the missing angle.

## Common Mistakes  
- **Wrong subtraction** – forgetting to subtract both 120° and 105° from 360°.  
- **Wrong angle values** – writing 235°, 150°, 125°, 110°, or 61° instead of 135°.  
- **Missing the degree symbol** – although “135” is fine, it’s clearer to write “135°”.

## Keep Practicing  
Double‑check that your final angle makes the total sum 360°. A quick mental check (120 + 105 + 135 = 360) can help you spot any slip‑ups. Keep up the good work!
